◆ What We Do

Youth Educated is a life-skills and mentorship platform for Kenyan youth. It combines an AI mentor named Amara, a human mentorship network, and 52 curriculum-aligned lessons. We built the platform to work offline because nearly half of Kenyans don't have reliable internet access.

! The Problem We Solve

Kenya spends a large portion of its national budget on education, but there are no standardized school counselors. The new curriculum requires schools to teach life skills, but most teachers don't have the tools or training to do so. With youth unemployment at 67%, over 11.6 million young Kenyans are navigating adulthood without structured guidance or mental health support.

★ What Makes Us Different
  • 🛡 Safeguarding System: We built a 4-tier child protection framework mapped to the Kenya Children Act. If a student uses crisis keywords, the system bypasses the AI and routes them to emergency contacts. It works even when offline. We named our AI mentor Amara, which means 'grace' in Swahili, to make sure the platform empowers girls as much as boys.
  • 📱 On-Device AI (Phase 2): The AI runs directly on the student's phone. This means they don't need an internet connection to use it. It works on the budget smartphones students already own, like the Tecno Spark or Samsung A-series. We are building this phase for under $6,000.
  • 🤝 Hybrid Mentorship: The AI handles routine daily guidance. This frees up our human mentors to focus on crisis intervention, career planning, and deep emotional support.
  • 💼 Hybrid Social Enterprise: Revenue from private academies and corporate ESG sponsors subsidizes free access for public schools. Our $15,150 pilot for 1,000 students is projected to generate $19,500 in Year 1 revenue — reaching self-sustainability within 12 months.
🇮🇹 Alignment with Società Benefit & Piano Mattei

Youth Educated is built to develop human capital in a measurable way. We track our impact using IRIS+ standards, which provides the verifiable data needed for Società Benefit annual reporting. We see our work as a natural fit for the Piano Mattei education pillar. It is an African-built AI solution to an African challenge, measured to European standards. We are looking for strategic partners and ecosystem integration, rather than just capital.
